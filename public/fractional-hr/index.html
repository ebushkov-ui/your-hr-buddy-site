How I work

Diagnose First, Then Build

Most companies start fixing their people problems before they understand what is actually broken. I run a focused diagnostic, write up what I find, and build against it. EOR and PEO off-ramps are where I get called most often.

Where I Come In

Series A and beyond, up to 500 employees, that have outgrown their people infrastructure.

The work usually lands with a founder, COO, CHRO, or CFO who knows something on the people side is broken but not what. You probably need me if one of these sounds familiar:

You need to get off a PEO or EOR. Started on Deel or Justworks for speed, now you need real entities and local payroll.

You are Series A or B with no HR foundation. Headcount climbing, no systems, no handbook, people decisions made without frameworks.

You are drowning in messy people data. Numbers living in too many places, none of them reliable, reporting that takes days.

You are hiring internationally with no infrastructure behind it. Offers going out in new countries before anyone checked the legal and cultural reality.

Your HRIS migration broke. A Rippling, Workday, or ChartHop rollout that stalled or never got finished.

You need a senior People leader but not a full-time hire. Someone to come in, diagnose, build, and hand back something that runs without them.
