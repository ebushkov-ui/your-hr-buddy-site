How I work

Diagnose First, Then Build

Most companies start fixing their people problems before they understand what is actually broken. I run a focused diagnostic, write up what I find, and build against it. EOR and PEO off-ramps are where I get called most often.

What To Send My Way

Series A and beyond, up to 500 employees, that have outgrown their people infrastructure.

The buyer is usually a founder, COO, CHRO, or CFO who knows something on the people side is broken but not what. Send someone my way when you hear:

A founder who needs to get off a PEO or EOR. Started on Deel or Justworks for speed, now needs real entities and local payroll.

A Series A or B with no HR foundation. Headcount climbing, no systems, no handbook, people decisions made without frameworks.

A company drowning in messy people data. Numbers living in too many places, none of them reliable, reporting that takes days.

An international hiring push with no infrastructure behind it. Offers going out in new countries before anyone checked the legal and cultural reality.

An HRIS migration that broke. A Rippling, Workday, or ChartHop rollout that stalled or never got finished.

A team that needs a senior People leader but not a full-time hire. Someone to come in, diagnose, build, and hand back something that runs without them.
